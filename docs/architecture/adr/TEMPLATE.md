## Short form (default)

Used by ADR-017 to ADR-032. One page: what was the situation, what was decided, what follows.

### 1. Title

One sentence: the question the decision answers.

### 2. Context

The situation and the forces that call for a decision.

### 3. Decision

What is decided, explicit and concise; subsections where there are several parts.

### 4. Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what is not decided or not built. State limits honestly.
